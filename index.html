Who keeps ending up here

You have a job, maybe a kid, and four hours a week to be a person.

Most servers are built for people with unlimited evenings. This one isn't. Half of us log on after bedtime, play for an hour, and go to sleep.

Somebody mentioned his four-year-old sister had broken her arm and was in hospital overnight. Three people asked how she was doing. He'd given her his Switch to keep her occupied. Fifteen minutes earlier the same channel was arguing about a Rickroll. Both of those are normal here.

Mostly late twenties through forties. Tired in a friendly way. Looking for company their own age.

How the house works

Astrid keeps the lamp lit.

It started as somewhere for a handful of internet friends to land, and grew outward. It's still run that way: a host, a guest list, and a door somebody is actually watching.

  • Queer and trans people are safe here. Not tolerated. Not welcome as long as it isn't mentioned. Safe. It's the one thing that gets you removed without a conversation first.
  • Bring arguments, not contempt. Disagree about anything. Be cruel about nothing.
  • Staff step in early and mean it. Somebody too drunk to be sensible gets put somewhere quiet until morning. It isn't a punishment and nobody holds it against you.
  • No pressure to perform. Nobody tracks whether you posted this week.
