The Server The Vote The Dragon The Age After The House

Everything here happened. It was pulled out of the server's own logs and the announcement channel, and none of it was decided by staff on their own — the two things that mattered most went to a vote, and the first one failed.

I. The Vote

The End had been sealed since the world began. On the second of August, Floor asked whether it was time.

2 Aug — "Open up the end?"
Yuh uh27%
Nuh uh73%
15 votes · rejected

Overwhelming vote against opening up the end dimension! Next vote coming up next week, or when people are up to it.

Floor · 3 August

It stayed shut. That is the part worth noticing — the question was put, the answer was no, and the answer held.

A week later, he asked again.

10 Aug — "Shall we open up the end at last?"
YAH82%
NOH18%
17 votes · carried

Two more people turned out for the second vote than the first. Same question, different answer, and nobody had to be talked into anything.

II. The Dragon

11 AUG

Version 1.2, and the seal breaks

Player head commands and miniblocks, new anti-grief software, daily automatic backups to private cloud, and serverwide entity culling. The End was unlocked the same day, with strict instructions not to go in yet.

11 AUG

The portal hub

A build went up at 1714, 72, -1017 — the only sanctioned way in. Enchanting table, grindstone, anvil, free lapis. And an armory: diamond armour, swords, shields, bows, arrows and blocks, free to take.

Nobody had to show up rich.

12 AUG

The date vote tied three ways

Five dates on the ballot, 22 votes cast, and three of them level at 23%. Floor called it for the 16th and told everyone not to panic about missing it — the dragon can always be brought back with four end crystals.

16 AUG

The dragon is designed by committee, that morning

Ten hours before the fight, Floor asked the channel how much health it should have. A vanilla dragon has 200.

Since there will be so many of us I feel it should have more then usual but not too much
— _cherriii.

i agree. will make it scale of how many players are fighting it.
— jongetje420

10:24am · the scaling health pool was Cherri's idea

By 10:28 it respawned crystals above 50% health. By 14:15 he had buffed its damage "ever so slightly more." Tundra's entire response was "Oh lort."

16 AUG

20:00 UTC+2

A waiting room in the End hub. A ten-minute warning, requested by Hayden and delivered by Floor. Free gear in the armory for anyone who turned up in iron.

We've got a lot of players online again. Im so stoked.

jongetje420 · six minutes to go

Not everyone made it. Masterchief was at work. Emerald was asleep. Irkie went six thousand blocks looking for an end city and found nothing.

Today is the day a new golden age of adventure and creativity rises. We will need help defeating this monstrous beast. We will wait for you before the fight starts.

Floor · the morning of

They killed it. There is a group photograph, taken in the End, and everyone who turned up is in it.

And that evening, the walls came down

The Minecraft server and the Discord were bridged the same night. Since then the two have been one room, which is why the rest of this page exists at all — everything after this point is on the record because people were talking in a channel that keeps them.

III. The Age After

What follows is a week of ordinary server life, which turns out to be the more interesting half.

With the End open, people went looking. Cherri came back with twelve shulker boxes and immediately needed more. Tundra had not been back since the fight and was starting to feel left behind.
