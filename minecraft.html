Your first night

Get whitelisted before anything else

Ask in the Discord. Until your name is on the list the server will not let you in, and nothing below matters.

Run /kit starter

Stone tools, food and torches. Once, so your first night is not spent punching a tree.

Run /rtp before you build anything

Drops you somewhere random and far out. Spawn is picked over; the good land is elsewhere.

Set a home the moment you pick a spot

/sethome base names it. Sleeping in a bed sets one automatically. You get three.

Claim your spot

Place a chest and the ground around it is claimed automatically. To do it properly, use the golden shovel.

Link your Discord

Run /discord link in game and send the four digits to @MothCraft Community. Chat then follows you off the server.

Claiming land

Everything you build is yours the moment you claim the ground under it. Inside a claim, nobody else can break blocks, open chests, use your beds or take from your farms — not because staff are watching, but because the server will not let them.

The fastest way — put down a chest

Place a chest anywhere unclaimed and the ground around it is claimed for you automatically. If all you want is a safe base, that is the entire process. No commands, no shovel, no reading further.

Doing it properly — the golden shovel

Hold a golden shovel

Any golden shovel works. Holding it puts you in claiming mode; putting it away takes you out.

Right-click one corner

Click the ground at one corner of the area you want. That sets your first point.

Right-click the opposite corner

The claim snaps into place and glowing blocks show the edges. Claims run bedrock to sky, so you never claim twice for a basement.

Adjust it later

Right-click a corner of an existing claim to drag it bigger or smaller. Nothing is permanent.

You start with 200 claim blocks. That is 200 blocks of surface area, not a 200×200 square — roughly a 14×14 base to begin with. Minimum claim size is 5×5, so you cannot fence off a single chest and call it a plot. You earn more the longer you play.

Letting people in

A claim locks everyone out by default, friends included. /trust <player> gives full build access, /containertrust allows chests and animals only, and /accesstrust allows doors and beds only. /untrust takes it back.

/abandonclaim returns a claim and its blocks to your pool. /claimslist shows everything you hold.

Keeping your stuff

Block logging — already running

Every block placed and broken is logged. If someone raids or griefs you, staff can see exactly who did what and roll it back. Report it with /helpop and do not rebuild over the damage first.

If you get griefed: leave it alone and report it. Rebuilding on top makes the rollback messier, and you may lose your own repairs along with the damage.

Logging is the backstop, not the first line. Claim your land and most of this never comes up.

GriefPrevention — the part that stops it happening

Rollbacks fix damage after the fact. Claims prevent it. Inside a claim the server physically refuses to let anyone else break a block, open a container, trample a crop or take an animal — there is nothing to report because there is nothing to undo.

Explosions are off inside claims

Creepers, beds in the Nether and stray TNT cannot crater your build. /claimexplosions toggles it if you actually want them, which you might for a working farm.

Trust is granular

Someone can be allowed to open your doors without being allowed to touch your chests. /trustlist shows exactly who has what on the claim you are standing in, and /untrust all clears the lot in one go.

Subdivide instead of over-trusting

/subdivideclaims carves a room out of your claim so a friend can build in that room and nowhere else. /restrictsubclaim makes it inherit nothing from the parent. /basicclaims puts the shovel back to normal.

Two commands worth remembering before you need them

/trapped gets you out of a claim you are stuck inside. /unlockdrops lets other people pick up your death pile so a friend can run it back to you.

Claims only cover what you claimed. The chest-placement shortcut protects a small area around the chest, not the whole base. If you have built outward since, run the golden shovel over the real footprint.

Ranks

There are two. Member is automatic — once you are whitelisted, it is already switched on the first time you log in. No application, no interview, no proving yourself for a fortnight. It includes homes, land claims, a starter kit and teleports.

Boost once. Keep it forever.

Booster applies within a minute of boosting the Discord. No ticket, no proof, no waiting on staff to notice. Then you get a second role that never comes off — so when you stop boosting, and you should stop boosting, everything stays yours. One month buys it permanently.
